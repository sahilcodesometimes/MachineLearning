# Understanding the Brain of AI 🧠

### Learning AI, one concept at a time.

## About This Journey

This repository is a record of my learning journey — a place where I document what I study, what I build, and everything I discover along the way.

I'm currently working toward a career in **Machine Learning**, dedicating time every day to learning, practicing, and strengthening my fundamentals. In the future, I aspire to become an **AI researcher and educator**.

But this journey isn't just about my own growth. I want to share everything I learn with people who are just getting started with AI or are on their own learning journey.

I believe **education should be free and accessible to everyone**. You shouldn't need expensive courses or exclusive resources to understand how AI works.

So, I'm starting here — learning in public, sharing my progress, and building a community where we can learn, grow, and explore the world of AI together.

**If you're learning AI too, you're welcome here. Let's figure it out together. 🚀**
