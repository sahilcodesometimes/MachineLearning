## My Guiding Principles

1. **Understand before memorizing.** Learn the mathematics and intuition behind algorithms.

3. **Build projects.** Apply concepts to real problems.
4. **Evaluate honestly.** Understand what works, what fails, and why.
5. **Share knowledge.** Learning should be accessible to everyone.
